# Prospective Entity-Selection Policy

Selection is structural and fixed before training.

An entity is eligible only if it has no duplicate timestamps, at least 14 days of coverage, at least 95% rows with all four finite and physically valid targets, nonconstant values for all four targets, a median interval within one second of 300 seconds, and a contiguous run of at least 2,016 observations with no gap above 450 seconds. Physical validity means CPU in [0,100] and the other three constructed targets nonnegative. Invalid rows are not repaired. Counts are generated by `01_dataset_audit/run_structural_audit.py`.

For each Bitbrains environment independently, rank eligible VM IDs by the ascending hexadecimal value of:

`SHA256("phase4a-v1|<environment>|<entity-id>")`

Select the first 35. The size is fixed and equal across fastStorage and Rnd, preventing the larger environment from dominating macro inference. No correlation, predictability, forecast error, or S1/S2/M1 outcome enters eligibility or ranking. If fewer than 35 remain after an independently discovered structural defect, stop and revise the protocol before training; do not backfill using outcome information.

For Materna secondary replication, take the intersection of structurally eligible IDs across all three traces, rank by `SHA256("phase4a-v1|Materna-shared|<entity-id>")`, and select the first 35. The current structural audit finds 398 IDs eligible in all three traces. This preserves a common VM cohort without treating later months as a pristine test set.

The exact selected IDs and their outcome-independent selection hashes are frozen in `SELECTED_ENTITY_COHORTS.csv` and `SELECTED_ENTITY_COHORTS_SUMMARY.json`. Their inclusion authorizes no scientific run; support and split identities still require a later pre-execution protocol checkpoint.
