# Phase 3D Research-Direction Decision

## Decision

**INCONCLUSIVE_POSTHOC_HETEROGENEITY**

## Audit answers

1. Stable cross-resource input benefit for any target: no.
2. Target-specific benefit: not established.
3. Horizon-specific benefit: see `outputs/gain_by_horizon.csv`; adjacent signs were required for every pattern label.
4. Machine-specific benefit: 4 stable and 6 partial S2-versus-S1 machines.
5. Regime-specific benefit: 0 stable and 10 partial controlled regimes.
6. Benefit concentration is recorded in `outputs/benefit_concentration.csv`; concentration cannot override stability requirements.
7. Training dependency associations are descriptive and do not establish that dependence predicts benefit causally.
8. M1 transfer is decomposed by regime and by the dedicated memory audit; the global Phase 3C label remains unchanged.
9. Loss share relationships are descriptive and cannot explain negative transfer causally.
10. Memory degradation breadth: [{'dimension': 'machines', 'positive_degradation_units': 7, 'total_units': 7}, {'dimension': 'folds', 'positive_degradation_units': 4, 'total_units': 5}, {'dimension': 'leads', 'positive_degradation_units': 8, 'total_units': 8}].
11. CPU multi-resource benefit over S1: assessed directly in S2-versus-S1 and M1-versus-S1 tables; M1-versus-S2 alone is not accepted as evidence.
12. Another multi-resource architecture is not justified without fresh confirmation.
13. Exact hypothesis: None survived the stable exploratory rule. Ten partial
    S2-versus-S1 regime patterns remain insufficient to define a confirmatory
    hypothesis.
14. Confirmation requires untouched temporal data, new TimeTrack telemetry, an external compatible dataset, or another independent source.

No training or architecture selection is authorized by this record.
