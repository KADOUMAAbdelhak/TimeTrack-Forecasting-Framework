# TimeTrack Multi-Resource Research Boundary

Date: 2026-09-28 (Europe/Helsinki)

## Status

**CLOSED PENDING INDEPENDENT DATA**

The current TimeTrack multi-resource architecture-development branch is closed
pending genuinely independent evidence. No further forecasting architecture
should be designed using the already inspected five outer folds as its
scientific justification.

This is a scoped research decision, not a claim that multi-resource forecasting
is universally impossible. It applies only to the current TimeTrack telemetry,
the selected CPU, throughput, memory, and disk-write targets, the chosen real
horizons, common-support construction, evaluated temporal period, tested plain
LSTM factorial, and the completed post-hoc stability analysis.

## Evidence chain

- **Phase 3A:** established that a four-resource study was technically feasible.
- **Phase 3B:** established the prospective plain-LSTM S1/S2/M1 factorial
  protocol and its reproducibility controls.
- **Phase 3C:** found that cross-resource input was not supported, shared
  representation was not supported, and the multi-resource forecasting premise
  was weak on the evaluated evidence.
- **Phase 3D:** found post-hoc heterogeneity and isolated positive pockets, but
  no controlled context-benefit pattern survived the predeclared stability rule
  across folds, machines, adjacent horizons, coverage, and both relative and
  absolute effects.

The Phase 3D decision is **INCONCLUSIVE_POSTHOC_HETEROGENEITY**. This does not
mean that every test cell lacks context benefit. It means that the observed
positive pockets are not sufficiently stable or broad to define a new
multi-resource forecasting hypothesis from this dataset.

Accordingly, the inspected evidence does not justify conditional gating,
target-specific context routing, selective cross-resource fusion, adaptive
sharing or task weighting, shared/private encoders, cross-resource attention,
or another multi-task architecture.

## Evaluation-independence requirement

Any future hypothesis or architecture informed by Phase 3C or Phase 3D is
post-hoc with respect to all five existing outer test folds. A future
confirmatory claim requires genuinely independent evidence, such as:

1. newly collected TimeTrack telemetry after the current observation period;
2. a previously untouched temporal extension;
3. an external compatible infrastructure or cloud-telemetry dataset; or
4. another independent deployment or workload source.

The existing five outer folds may be reused only for exploratory analysis,
debugging, or mechanism development that is explicitly labelled post-hoc. They
may not be represented as pristine confirmatory evaluation for a method designed
after inspection of Phase 3C or Phase 3D.

## Next research action

The next recommended phase is **INDEPENDENT DATA / RESEARCH-SCOPE AUDIT**, with
no model training. That phase should determine whether additional TimeTrack or
OAI telemetry, an untouched later period, or compatible external telemetry can
be obtained; whether the existing findings support a publishable benchmark or
boundary study without inventing another model; whether independent data could
support a genuinely prospective hypothesis; and whether the project should
pivot away from multi-resource forecasting.

Those questions are intentionally not answered by this freeze record.
