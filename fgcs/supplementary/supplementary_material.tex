\subsection*{Atomic comparisons and support rules}
Atomic units preserve hierarchy $\times$ model $\times$ reconciliation method
$\times$ output width $\times$ fold (and seed for stochastic robustness).
Support classes combine relative-effect direction, direct bootstrap CI, fold
consistency, output-width consistency, seed consistency where applicable, and
top/bottom trade-off labels; seed-mean pooling alone cannot upgrade a reversed
seed (see \texttt{docs/FINAL\_STATISTICAL\_PROTOCOL.md} and
\texttt{configs/final\_robustness\_statistics.yaml}).

\section{Seed policy}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Seeds $\{0,1,2\}$ for LightGBM and DLinear robustness checks.
  \item Python/\texttt{random} and NumPy seeds follow the frozen runner policy
    for each claim-generating pack.
  \item DLinear calls \texttt{torch.manual\_seed(seed)} at fit time and retains
    seed-dependent training variation.
  \item LightGBM receives \texttt{random\_state=seed} with library-default
    \texttt{subsample}=1.0 (not overridden); frozen predictions are bitwise
    identical across seeds and are reported as invariance, not independent
    replicates.
  \item Thread settings: DLinear uses one thread in the frozen budget; LightGBM
    uses \texttt{n\_jobs=-1}.
\end{itemize}
