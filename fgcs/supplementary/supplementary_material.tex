\section{Purpose}
This supplement collects seed-by-fold grids, extended peak matrices, efficiency
fields, and provenance pointers omitted from the main paper for page limits.
All numerical claims in the main text are sourced from the frozen aggregate under
\texttt{final-reporting-freeze-v2}.

\section{Extended seed grids}
Full LightGBM/DLinear seed-by-fold-by-horizon tables are provided as CSV snapshots
in \texttt{results/main/table05\_seed\_robustness.csv}.

\texttt{results/main/table08\_peak\_operational\_evidence.csv}.

\section{Statistical families}
