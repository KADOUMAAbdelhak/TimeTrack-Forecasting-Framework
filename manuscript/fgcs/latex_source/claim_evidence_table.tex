Its direction recurs across backbones
  & Tier~2: ratio above one in 30 of 32 backbone--resource--block cells; for the three non-LSTM backbones,

  & Tier-2 S2:S1 ratios between 0.981 and 1.035; no context effect holds for every backbone; external memory is

  & In the shared LSTM encoder, median excess sign-conflict rate $-0.037$ and $-0.021$ and median absolute

  & Amendment A1: uncertainty weighting passed its manipulation check and beat equal-loss M1 in 8 of 8 cells, by
    at most 0.4\%, yet stayed above S2 in all 8 (Table~\ref{tab:a1}); GradNorm failed its check in one block.
  & LSTM only; associations on 20 VMs in two blocks; A1's hypothesis was chosen after the diagnostics were seen. \\

  & Tier-1 M1 uses about 74.5\% fewer parameters and 75\% fewer models than S2; tier-2 shared LSTM and

  & Amendment A3: inner validation ranks the sharing cost in \AiiiDetects{} of \AiiiFrames{} settings; a 1\%

    Supplementary Section~S-H).
  & No production latency, energy or cost savings were measured; A3 is post hoc on components already seen;
    grouping was tested for the LSTM only. \\
