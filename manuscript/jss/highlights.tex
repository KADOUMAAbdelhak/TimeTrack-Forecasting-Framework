\begin{itemize}
  \item A 2x2 design separates cross-resource input from representation sharing.
  \item Context gains are small and depend on resource, environment and backbone.
  \item All-input sharing lowered accuracy in 29 of 32 cells across four backbones.
  \item Shared LSTM gradients were unequal in magnitude, not excessively conflicting.
  \item Most shared models need a quarter of the parameters, at an accuracy cost.
\end{itemize}
